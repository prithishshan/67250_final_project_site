The People Changing the World Need Backup.

3 in 4 nonprofits are dealing with persistent job vacancies. We built CivicMatch to serve as a bridge between the skills of cherished community members and the organizations running on empty.


It's hard for organizations to find the right volunteers.

Surveys showed, 35% of organizations explicitly report that it is challenging to attract volunteers who have the specific, specialized skills they actually need for their projects.


Currently, the right matches aren't being made.

The nonprofit sector experiences an incredibly high turnover rate of 19% (compared to roughly 12% in the corporate sector), and 95% of nonprofit leaders report being highly concerned about staff burnout. This churn rate can be a death sentence for struggling non-profits doing great work.
